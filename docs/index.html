How it works

One script tag. Continuous protection.

DeepCheck watches behavior, not identity — scoring every session as it happens, from the first mouse move to the final click on “Pay”.

1

Embed the SDK

A 6 KB script collects behavioral signals in the browser — mouse trajectories, click timing, scroll rhythm, typing cadence, hesitation gaps — and streams them every 2 seconds.

2

The ensemble decides

Random Forest, Isolation Forest, and an LSTM sequence model each score the session; their weighted blend becomes a single fraud probability, robust to single-signal tricks.

3

Act on the score

Your app gets the score, label, and SHAP explanation in real time — let genuine users through, challenge the suspicious, block confirmed bots automatically.

Why DeepCheck

Security that customers never see.

Every defense your risk team wants, none of the friction your users hate.

Zero friction

No CAPTCHAs, no puzzles, no extra taps. Real users are verified silently by the way they naturally behave.

Real-time decisions

Scores update every 2 seconds during the session, with model scoring measured at a 42 ms median — fast enough to gate the payment button itself.

Explainable by design

Every score ships with its SHAP feature attribution — your fraud team and your regulator both see exactly why a session was flagged.

Privacy-preserving

The SDK records interaction timing only — never key content, never field values, never card data. Nothing sensitive ever leaves the page.

Resists smart bots

Trained against Selenium-style automation that simulates human motion — robotic regularity is detected per channel, so one faked signal is never enough.

SOC dashboard included

A live operations view of every session — risk history charts, top SHAP drivers, and one-glance triage sorted by severity.
